Mobile Network (GSM) TDM Simulator with CRC and Noise

One tower, one radio channel, 8 time slots. Press the button to send one frame. The tower adds CRC bits, the channel may add noise, and the receiver checks the frame.

1. Phones (tap a phone to connect or disconnect it)

The text under each phone is what that phone received after the CRC check of the last frame.

2. Frame on the shared radio channel

Each connected phone sends 4 bits in its own slot. An empty slot is filled with 0000 and counted as wasted.

3. CRC and noise check

Press Send one frame to see the CRC check.

4. Log

How to read this: The tower mixes the 8 slots into one frame (TDM). It divides the frame by the generator 1011 and attaches the 3-bit remainder as the CRC. If noise is on, one bit is flipped on the way. The receiver divides again: a zero remainder means the frame is accepted and each phone gets its data, and a non-zero remainder means the frame is rejected and nobody gets wrong data. Disconnected phones leave empty slots, which are wasted.
